# Usability Survey

Questions for the Google Form that students fill out after using Course Atlas. Ask each participant to spend about 15 minutes with the app first: create a class, upload one or two real lecture decks, ask at least five questions, and mark a few weak topics.

## About you

1. What year are you in? (Freshman / Sophomore / Junior / Senior / Graduate)
2. Which class did you upload materials for? (short answer)
3. How do you usually study from lecture slides? (Reread slides / Search the PDF / Ask classmates / Use ChatGPT or similar / Other)

## Ratings

Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree, 5 = Strongly agree.

4. The answers were correct for my course.
5. The answers used my course's material, not generic information.
6. The citations pointed to the slide or page that actually covered my question.
7. Seeing the cited page helped me trust the answer.
8. The topics the app found matched what my lecture covered.
9. Marking weak topics and seeing their pages helped me decide what to review.
10. The "explain this topic" follow-up was useful.
11. The app was easy to use without instructions.
12. Answers came back fast enough.
13. I would use Course Atlas to study for an exam.

## Comparison

14. Compared to how you normally study, Course Atlas was: (Much worse / Worse / About the same / Better / Much better)
15. Compared to asking ChatGPT, Course Atlas was: (Much worse / Worse / About the same / Better / Much better / I don't use ChatGPT)

## Open questions

16. Describe an answer that was wrong or unhelpful, if any.
17. What was the most useful part of the app?
18. What was the most frustrating part?
19. What one feature would make you use it more?
